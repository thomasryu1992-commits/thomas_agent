# G6-buy buys global equity in dollars: Thomas exchanges in the app, the fire spends it by amount

- **Why:** global equity (target 30) has US-listed candidates (Q3 2026-10-07: the ordinary account holds it
  overseas direct, for the 2.5M deduction). Until now the draft left it out.
  - Toss's Open API never converts KRW: its FAQ says an order short of dollars is refused with `422
    insufficient-buying-power`. There is no exchange endpoint.
  - The pension account is not exposed either (`/api/v1/accounts` returns `BROKERAGE` only).
  - Thomas chose the dollar path over switching to a domestic-listed ETF ("승인: G6 글로벌 주식 달러 경로(…)
    (Thomas 2026-10-27)"). The record is `G6_BUY_CASH_AUTOINVEST_V0.1.md` §2g.
- **The draft** (`autoinvest_proposal`): a slot whose first candidate is overseas is a dollar slot.
  - It fills in 10,000 KRW steps of exposure, from the USD cash (less 3%) first. After that it takes from the
    KRW budget as an exchange to make, which comes out of the same KRW pool the domestic shares use.
  - The dollars there are become one amount item. The exchange amount is shown on Thomas's terminal only.
  - Telegram says an exchange is needed, never how much. A draft with nothing to order but an exchange is
    `exchange_only`, and it cannot be approved.
- **The order** (`toss_orders.buy_us_amount`): a US MARKET BUY by `orderAmount`, with the ticker and the
  amount (at most two decimals) checked before any POST. The adapter's public surface is now these two buys.
  There is still no sell, modify or cancel, and side is still `BUY`.
- **The executor** (`autoinvest`):
  - A US item (`amount_usd`) is placed once per US regular session, from five minutes after the open to
    seventy minutes before the close. The calendar comes from `TossHoldingsFeed.us_regular_sessions`.
  - Checks before placing: an ACTIVE ETF, the rate, the scenario and daily caps in KRW, and USD cash
    (`insufficient_usd` holds).
  - It is done once anything filled; one closed with nothing filled goes again next session.
  - Domestic and US items of one scenario each go in their own session. The cap counts an open US order at its
    KRW estimate.
- **Not known yet:** Toss's exchange spread, and whether a US market amount order reserves more than its
  amount. A domestic market order reserved at the upper limit; the first live run will show the US case.
- **Checked:**
  - The draft's pools: USD first, partial USD, exchange-only, the exchange inside the KRW pool, the US fee.
  - The notices carry no amount.
  - The executor:
    - in and out of the session, including the 70-minute margin;
    - once per session, with the re-order after an unfilled close;
    - insufficient USD, and the cap in KRW;
    - a mixed scenario, an open US order under a domestic check, and the terminal status.
  - The feed's new reads.
  - 18 mutations were each caught.
