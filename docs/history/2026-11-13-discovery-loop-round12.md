**Why this is registered at all.** The form says a hypothesis that cannot fill its first three rows does not go
up. This one fills them, weakly: its payer is behaviour (capital that rotates late between Bitcoin and alts), not
structure, and nothing stops the trade being competed away. The registration quotes the rule and says it was read
as filled-but-weak. The three payers the data on disk could name by structure, listings, forced liquidations and
funding (and H-R1's sellers in a hurry), were spent in rounds 8 to 11, and no further one was found. If the next hypothesis can only write its second row this way too, that
exhaustion is what gets reported.

**Choices that are not obvious.**

- *The signal is read on members fixed before its window.* The month's top thirty would lean the sign to "alts
  up" after every refresh: a coin enters on a volume surge, and a surge follows a run-up. A test pins it with a
  coin that enters after its own run.
- *The null is the real sides moved in time*, every shift from 8 decisions to 8 short of a full turn. That is
  about 240 books, not 300, and neighbours are near-copies, so the number of runs is printed beside the p.
- *Judged at the stressed cost although the ladder judged top-thirty books at the engine's.* The whole book turns
  over exactly when the spread's trend breaks. A book that passes only at the engine's cost is read as a
  cost-dependent lead with one later re-read against the spread record, as H-R1's table had it.
- *A pass would not buy a confirm read.* The nearest record, the regime set's rotation pair B09 and D08, was read
  on 2020-10 through 2026-10, and the direction of alts against Bitcoin by year is public market history for
  2025-26 as much as for the development years.
- *The counts pass prints no side and no date*, only how many decisions have a side, how often it turns and how
  long it lasts. The size line is then filled with arithmetic on the turn count alone; round 11's post-counts
  guess about the return was wrong.
- *Each leg is printed by year.* Rounds 10 and 11 each had a result whose reading changed once the hedge leg was
  separated.
