# D8's lane-evidence review leaves the locked MVP role out, and reads blog lanes by outcome

- **Decision (Thomas, 2026-10-06):** scorecard Q4 (a) (`docs/proposals/SYSTEM_SCORECARD_V0.1.md` §4.2).
- **Why it needed saying:** D8 (2026-09-26) reviews for removal, on 2026-11-25, any non-crypto lane without
  evidence of use and quality. The scorecard measured `general.specialist` — the CLI's "analyze this business
  idea" — at one run in seven days, while the blog engine ran 104 and Hermes-dispatched research 56. Read
  literally, D8 would put the locked MVP role up for removal; it is a core role, not a removable lane.
- **What it says now** (`SYSTEM_REVIEW_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN_V0.1.md`, decision section): `general.specialist` is
  out of D8's scope; the review looks at the content, research, knowledge and Hermes lanes; a blog lane's
  quality evidence is impressions and clicks, not run counts — the Tistory reading on 2026-10-31 is the first —
  and the disabled Naver weekly row is removed or revived at the same review.
- **What it does not do:** no code; the locked MVP use case is unchanged.
